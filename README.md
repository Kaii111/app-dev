# Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba – Infinity Castle

## Movie Introduction

**Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle** is an action-packed anime movie that continues the journey of *Tanjiro Kamado* and the Demon Slayer Corps as they face powerful enemies inside the mysterious Infinity Castle.

> No matter how difficult the battle becomes, never give up on the people you want to protect.

---

## Main Characters

### Demon Slayers

1. **Tanjiro Kamado** – A kind-hearted swordsman who fights to save humanity.
2. **Zenitsu Agatsuma** – A fighter who uses Thunder Breathing.
3. **Inosuke Hashibira** – A fearless demon slayer who uses Beast Breathing.
4. **Giyu Tomioka** – The Water Hashira.
5. **Kyojuro Rengoku** – The Flame Hashira.
